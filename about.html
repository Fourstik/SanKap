Our story

About SanKap

A student-built restaurant finder for Pampanga — made with local pride, a lot of coffee, and a genuine love for good food.

What is SanKap?

Pampanga's dining directory, built local.

SanKap is a MongoDB-powered restaurant finder designed specifically for diners across Angeles City, Clark, San Fernando, and Mabalacat. The name comes from the Filipino question "Saan, Kap?" — meaning "Where to, boss?" — a nod to our Kapampangan roots.

Whether you're craving sisig in Angeles City or sushi in Clark, SanKap helps you find exactly where to go — filtered by cuisine, location, and rating.

Our motivation

Why we built this

Pampanga is known as the culinary capital of the Philippines, yet finding a reliable, organized directory of its restaurants has always been a challenge for locals and visitors alike. We wanted to change that. SanKap was built as our answer — a simple, fast, and locally-focused platform that puts Pampanga's best dining spots at your fingertips.

"Saan, Kap? — the question every hungry Kapampangan asks."
